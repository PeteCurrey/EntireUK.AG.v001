# Entire UK Land Radar — Artificial Intelligence Boundary & Governance

## 1. The Core AI Principle

> **AI is an analytical accelerator, NOT an authoritative source of factual land data.**

In the Entire UK technology stack, artificial intelligence is strictly bounded to prevent hallucination, legal risk, and ungrounded commercial appraisals.

---

## 2. Permitted AI Capabilities

AI systems (LLMs, natural language models, document summarizers) are **permitted** to:
1. **Summarise Evidence**: Generate human-readable briefs combining verified signals and constraints.
2. **Explain Signals**: Translate complex spatial metrics (e.g. "centroid 420m from boundary") into clear operational language for acquisition managers.
3. **Draft Diligence Questions**: Identify gaps in the evidence record (e.g., "Access title unconfirmed; query Land Registry regarding easement").
4. **Compare Evidence**: Cross-reference conflicting historical planning application descriptions.
5. **Assist Human Analysts**: Format internal investment memorandums based exclusively on verified database facts.

---

## 3. Explicit AI Prohibitions

AI models are **strictly forbidden** from independently declaring or inventing:
- **Planning Permission**: AI cannot assert that planning permission exists or will be granted.
- **Ownership & Title**: AI cannot assert legal ownership without Land Registry deed confirmation.
- **Cadastral Boundaries**: AI cannot invent or hallucinate site polygons.
- **Development Capacity**: Unit numbers and density assumptions must stem from explicit planning formulas, not generative guesses.
- **Valuation & GDV**: AI cannot produce automated valuations or Gross Development Values without audited transactional comparables and formal appraisal formulas.
- **Acquisition Decisions**: AI cannot authorize or recommend binding financial transactions.

Every AI-generated statement must cite its underlying database provenance reference.
